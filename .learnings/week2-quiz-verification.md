### Q1: NPV 定义
**Pearson:** "The net present value of a particular investment project represents:"  
**Week 2:** "The net present value of a particular investment project represents:"

**状态:** ✅ 题目和答案完全一致

### Q2: PV 计算
**Pearson:** "A business is considering an investment that will generate a cash flow in exactly five year's time. The discount rate associated with the investment is 15% per annum. To deduce the present value of the projected cash flow, we have to multiply the amount of the cash flow to be received in five years' time by:"  
**Week 2:** "To find the PV of a cash flow in exactly 5 years at 15% p.a., multiply the cash flow by:"  
**答案:** "1/(1.15)⁵"  
**状态:** ✅ 题目简化但含义相同，答案一致

### Q3: Annuity Table
**Pearson:** "In an annuity table, the figure which corresponds to five years and 10% represents:"  
**Week 2:** "In an annuity table, the figure for 5 years and 10% represents:"  
**答案:** "the present value of £1 receivable/payable at the end of each of the next five years."  
**状态:** ✅ 题目简化，答案一致

### Q4: 折现率低估
**Pearson:** "You have just assessed a project, involving an immediate cash outflow followed by a series of cash inflows over the next seven years, by deducing the NPV and the IRR. You have now discovered that you have underestimated the discount rate. Correcting for the underestimation will have the following effects..."  
**Week 2:** "You underestimated the discount rate. Correcting this will:" + context  
**答案:** NPV reduce, IRR no change  
**状态:** ✅ 题目简化，答案一致

### Q5: IRR 定义
**Pearson:** "The internal rate of return can best be described as:"  
**Week 2:** "The internal rate of return can best be described as:"  
**答案:** "the discount rate at which a set of cash flows have a zero net present value."  
**状态:** ✅ 完全一致

### Q6: IRR 假设

**Week 2:** "When using IRR, an assumption is made which is not usually valid. This assumption is that:"  
**答案:** "funds released by the project can be reinvested by the business at the IRR of the project."  
**状态:** ✅ 题目简化，答案一致
